I've been meaning to start a blog for a while.

Not because I think I have all the answers. Honestly, the opposite — I'm in the middle of figuring a lot of things out, and writing has always helped me think more clearly.

I'm Dexter. I build websites for local businesses here in Fort Wayne, Indiana, and I'm working on a handful of my own digital products on the side. The day-to-day is a mix of client calls, code, design decisions, and the occasional existential question about whether I picked the right tech stack.

## What I'll Write About

A few things I keep coming back to:

**Building for local businesses.** Most of my clients run a service — lawn care, barbershops, auto shops. They're great at what they do and terrible at being found online. I want to write about what actually works for small businesses, without the generic advice that fills most marketing blogs.

**Product development.** I'm building [FitQuest](https://github.com/dextercarlmiller/fitquest), a fitness app, and [Fort Wayne Businesses](https://github.com/dextercarlmiller/fortwaynebusinesses.com), a local business directory. Both are labors of love. I'll share what goes wrong, what goes right, and the decisions behind them.

**The Fort Wayne angle.** I care about this city. I think there's more opportunity here than most people realize, and I want to write about building something meaningful in a mid-sized Midwest city — not Silicon Valley, not a major metro.

**Honest technical stuff.** I'm not going to write "10 JavaScript tips you won't believe" posts. But when I learn something genuinely useful — a pattern, a mistake, a tool — I'll write it up.

## Why Now

Partly because I finally set up this blog (you're reading it), and partly because I feel like I'm at an interesting inflection point. The studio is growing. The products are getting real. I want to document this chapter while I'm in it.

No promises on frequency. But I'm going to write when I have something worth saying.

Thanks for being here.

— Dexter
